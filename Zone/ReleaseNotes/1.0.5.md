# 1.0.5
date: 2026-11-17
summary: My words you can edit at a glance, thousands more words, dictation that learns your fixes, and an updated speech engine.

## My words, one key per word
symbol: character.book.closed
where: Settings > My words
brief: Each word is its own key: tap to fix its spelling, x to remove it.

My words shows each word as its own key. Add from one field, tap a word to fix its spelling, tap the x to remove it, and changes save as you make them. Words Zone learned from your corrections are listed apart, so a wrong one is easy to spot and remove.

## Thousands more names and words
symbol: textformat.abc
brief: Teams, players, places, brands and tech terms are kept as you type them.

Typing and swipe typing know thousands more names and words: sports teams and players, places, brands, tech terms and British spellings. They are kept as you type them, names take their capitals, and brands written with a hyphen or apostrophe, like T-Mobile and McDonald's, come out right when typed without one.

## Dictation learns your fixes
symbol: text.badge.checkmark
where: Settings > My words
brief: Retype the last word of a dictation and Zone writes it your way next time.

When you backspace over the last word of a dictation and type it the way you meant, Zone adds it to My words and writes it that way in your next dictations. Words learned this way are listed apart in My words, so a wrong one is easy to remove. Turn off Learn from corrections to stop it.

## An updated speech engine
symbol: waveform
brief: Fixes for long dictations, model downloads and meeting memory use.

The speech engine is updated, with fixes for long dictations, model downloads and memory use while a meeting works out who is speaking. Dictations and meeting transcripts should read the same as before. If anything got worse, tell us.

## Quick after a pause
symbol: bolt
brief: The first dictation after the phone sat idle no longer waits.

The first dictation after your phone has sat idle finishes about as fast as any other. Zone starts reading the speech model back into memory as soon as the keyboard opens, and finishes while you talk, instead of making you wait up to 3 seconds after you stop.

## Long dictations keep their periods
symbol: text.alignleft
brief: A pause between sentences keeps its period, never a doubled one.

Long dictations with Fluid Speech Mini keep the period where you paused between sentences, and no longer write a doubled period there.

## Voice notes that stay out of the way
symbol: note.text
brief: A note records in a small card, and you can edit or delete it once it saves.

Press the Action Button and the note records in a card at the top of Zone, not a screen in front of it. Once it saves, edit, copy, share or delete it right there, and Added to Notes takes you to it on Home. Press again and the next note starts at once.

## Lighter Notes and History
symbol: list.bullet
where: Home
brief: Notes, History and Meetings are lists, with Copy one tap away.

Notes, History and Meetings show each item as a row on the page instead of a card of its own, with two lines of it and Copy beside it. Tap a dictation to read all of it. Deleting asks first, and a swipe in History no longer deletes by itself.

## Start and stop from the Dynamic Island
symbol: mic.circle
where: Settings > Dynamic Island
brief: Hold the island to dictate, take a note, pause or stop.

Hold the island while Zone is ready and tap Dictate or Note. Dictate copies your words, ready to paste with any keyboard. While you record, Pause is on the left and Stop on the right, and Stop is on the Lock Screen too. While Zone waits, the island and the Lock Screen show how many dictations and words you have kept since you turned it on.

## Finish when quiet
symbol: timer
where: Settings > Microphone and timing > Finish when quiet
brief: Stop talking and Zone types for you, no tap needed. Off until you turn it on.

Turn on Finish when quiet and a dictation ends by itself after you go quiet for the time you choose, from 10 seconds to 2 minutes. Zone waits for your first word, and any sound starts the wait over, so a pause to think does not end it. A short walkthrough shows how it works when you turn it on.

## Tap out your next word
symbol: text.cursor
where: Settings > Keyboard > Suggestions
brief: Finish a word and the row above the keys offers the next one.

Finish a word and the row above the keys offers three words likely to come next. Tap one to write it with a space, and keep tapping to carry the sentence on. While you type, the row puts the word that fits the sentence first, so "good m" offers morning. Suggestions are now on unless you turned them off.

## Hold Zone's icon
symbol: apps.iphone
where: Home Screen
brief: Start a voice note, a dictation to copy or a meeting right from Zone's icon.

Hold Zone's icon on the Home Screen for Voice note, Dictate and copy, Record a meeting and Share Zone with a friend. Under your word count, Home says what it adds up to, like 1.2 Great Gatsbys, and a tap shows more. Hold the gear on Home to jump to My words, Speech model, Keyboard, Appearance or Backup.

## Tell me how it's going
symbol: envelope
where: Settings > Send feedback
brief: Say or type what you think. It goes out as an email you send yourself.

Say or type what works and what doesn't, and Zone writes it into an email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent until you send it there. Home also marks word milestones, starting at 500 words, with a short note the next time you open Zone. Answer it or close it.

## Also in this version

- Deleting data in Settings > Your data now removes saved Cleanup originals and their cleaned text for the dates you choose.
- One speech model, Fluid Speech Mini, on every iPhone, explained step by step in Settings > Speech model. Light is removed. Zone now needs iOS 18.
- On iOS 26, removing Fluid Speech Mini frees all 1.2 GB it prepared, and a nearly full iPhone no longer refuses to load a speech model it already has.
- Buttons are rounded keys like the keyboard's, from Back and Done at the top of each screen to Turn on, and cards line up edge to edge. Swiping back still works.
- Shadows under cards and keys follow the real sky: they lean right in the morning and left in the afternoon, and the moon casts them after dark.
- Dictation writes a few brand names it used to mishear when the sentence is about them, like Vercel when you talk about deploying a site.
- A single mistyped letter in a newer everyday word or app name is now corrected, and a two-letter slip is fixed when the tap landed on the edge nearest the intended key.
- Zone keeps its dot in the Dynamic Island when a call, Siri or other audio briefly takes the microphone, instead of the orange microphone pill coming back.
- The keyboard mic recovers after an interrupted meeting. Tapping twice while it starts records once.
- A new dictation after a question starts with a capital in fields that use sentence capitals. Correcting an unsure word checks that the cursor is still at the dictation.
- Sending everything to a paired computer again includes deletions made while it was offline.
- The swipe typing trail is shorter and a little more see-through, so it shows the path without covering the keys.
- The keyboard does less repeated work each time it opens, so the first swipe lands sooner.
- Phone number and card number fields on web forms get no swiped words or corrections, as code and password fields already did.
- Tap the Z on the keyboard, or hold the microphone, to choose the style for your next dictation. The styles open in the row above the keys.
- The recent dictations button sits on the right of the keyboard, beside the microphone.
- The keyboard's Paste key pastes without iPhone asking you to allow it each time.
- Verification code fields open on your next keyboard, where iPhone offers the code from Messages.
- Autocorrect leaves a word after @ or # as you typed it, and corrects a word typed right after a dictation.
- Your text replacements expand even when the shortcut is also a word Zone knows.
- Dictation leaves out stutters: a sound you cut off before the word, a small word said twice by accident and a phrase you started over.
- The 123 key is as wide as Return when no emoji key sits beside it.
- After Dictate and copy from the Action Button, opening Zone later shows Home instead of that earlier dictation's finished screen.
- A typo made while fixing a word by hand is no longer saved to My words.
- Tightened wording across setup, Home, Settings, Cleanup, Meetings and the keyboard.
- The keyboard's rows sit level with Apple's instead of pressed low against the globe row.
- Dictation no longer swaps an everyday word like "repo" for one of My words that sounds like it.
- The Dynamic Island's edge lights only while Zone is recording, and a check shows for a moment when a recording is kept.
- Words per minute counts only the time you spend talking, so short replies no longer lower it. The island's check shows each dictation's speed.
- With CarPlay connected, Zone uses the microphone only while you record, so music and audiobooks stay on the car's speakers in stereo.
- A word typed in the middle of a sentence in Messages no longer gets a capital it should not have.
- Dictation style names start with a capital: Casual, Very casual, Formal and Excited.
- A new voice graphic, Bars: thin upright bars that rise as you talk, on the keyboard and on Home. Choose it in Settings > Appearance > Voice graphic.
- Home shows your longest dictation under your stats, how long it ran and how many words it held, once one runs a minute or more. Dictations already in History count.
- Seven more voice graphics in Settings > Appearance: Scope (from Hex), Ink, Glyphs, Branch, Inkblot, Lanes and Stars. Each draws your voice a different way.
- Casual and Very casual write times the way people text them, like 7 am and 12:45 pm, and a time after "later than" or "earlier than" is written as a time.
- A dictated question no longer ends with a stray period after its question mark.
- Recent dictations stays in the row above the keys while word suggestions show, so a dictation that did not land is always one tap away.
- The style you pick with the Z stays until you pick another, there or in Settings > Text style.
- Zone's icon has an ice blue dot in this version. The dot takes the next accent color with each version.
- In Beta: hold the space bar to talk and let go to type it. Turn it on in Settings > Keyboard > Hold space. A tap still types a space, and sliding still moves the cursor.
- Settings > About has Rate Zone on the App Store, for when you feel like it.
- After an iPhone restart, Zone opens with your own color, sounds and notes. Before, it could open on the defaults with an empty notes list until you closed it.
- History opens and refreshes faster with a long library, and shows how much text you have kept and how little space it takes.
- With the microphone kept ready, Zone writes to storage about 95% less often in the background.
