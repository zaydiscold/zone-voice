# 1.0.5
date: 2026-11-17
summary: Zone learns your fixes, offers your next word, and records voice notes in a small card.

## Dictation
symbol: waveform

* Finish when quiet: Let dictation end after 10 seconds to 2 minutes of quiet. Set it in Microphone and timing.
* Learns your fixes: Retype the last word of a dictation and Zone writes it your way next time.
- Styles on the keyboard: Tap the Z or hold the mic to pick a style. It stays until you change it.
- Casual texts like you do: Casual and Very casual write times like 7 am and 12:45 pm.
- No more stutters: Cut-off sounds, doubled words and restarted phrases are left out.
- Hold space to talk (Beta): Hold the space bar, speak, let go. Turn it on in Settings > Keyboard > Hold space.
- Cleaner punctuation: Long dictations keep their periods, and questions end with one mark.
- More words it knows: Zone hears AI and developer words, and brands like Vercel where they fit.
- Everyday words stay: "Repo" is no longer swapped for one of My words that sounds like it.
- Speed counts talking only: Words per minute ignores pauses, so short replies no longer lower it.
- One speech model: Every iPhone uses Fluid Speech Mini. Light is gone, and Zone now needs iOS 18.
- Sentence starts: Dictate after a question and it starts with a capital, in fields that capitalize sentences.
- Unsure words: Fixing an unsure word first checks that the dictation is still at the cursor.
- Style names: Casual, Very casual, Formal and Excited start with a capital.

## Keyboard
symbol: keyboard

* Next word: Finish a word and tap one of three likely next words.
- Thousands more words: Teams, players, places, brands and British spellings, with their capitals.
- My words, one key per word: Tap a word to fix it, or x to remove it. Learned words have their own list.
- Smarter typo fixes: One wrong letter in a newer word gets fixed. Short words too, if you tapped near the key.
- No learned typos: A typo made while fixing a word by hand is no longer saved to My words.
- Paste without asking: iPhone stops asking each time. Turn it on in Settings > Keyboard > Paste key.
- Codes from Messages: A code field opens your next keyboard, where iPhone offers the code.
- Private fields: Phone and card number fields get no swiped words or corrections.
- Recent dictations: The button sits beside the mic and stays while suggestions show.
- Autocorrect knows its place: Words after @ or # stay as typed, and text replacements always expand.
- Capitals in Messages: A word typed mid-sentence no longer gets a stray capital.
- Layout: The rows line up with Apple's keyboard. Without an emoji key, 123 is as wide as Return.
- Swipe trail: Shorter and more see-through, so it never covers the keys.

## Voice notes
symbol: note.text

* Notes in a card: The Action Button records a note in a small card, not a full screen.
- Edit right after: After a note saves, you can edit, copy, share or delete it right there.

## Dynamic Island
symbol: capsule

* Start and stop from the island: Hold the island to dictate, take a note, pause or stop.
- Session at a glance: Hold the island to see dictations and words. The Lock Screen shows speed and time saved.
- Stays put: Zone keeps its dot after a call or Siri.
- Lights only when recording: The edge glows only while Zone records. A check shows words and speed.

## Home and library
symbol: house

- Lighter lists: Notes, History and Meetings are simple rows with Copy beside them. Delete asks first.
- Hold Zone's icon: Start a note, a dictation or a meeting from the Home Screen.
- Word milestones: Home marks 500 words and up, with comparisons like 1.2 Great Gatsbys.
- Longest dictation: Home shows your longest dictation once one runs a minute or more.
- Send feedback: Tap Send feedback in Settings, then say or type it. It opens as an email for you to send.
- Shortcuts from the gear: Hold the gear on Home to jump to My words, Keyboard and more.
- Rate Zone: In Settings under About, for when you feel like it.
- Home after a copy: After Dictate and copy, Zone opens to Home, not the old dictation.
- Computers: Send everything again also deletes, on the computer, what you deleted while it was offline.
- Delete means delete: Settings > Delete data now also clears text files left by earlier versions.

## Look and feel
symbol: paintpalette

- Eight new voice graphics: Bars, Scope, Ink, Glyphs, Branch, Inkblot, Lanes and Stars.
- One shape: Buttons are rounded keys like the keyboard's. Swiping back still works.
- Real shadows: They lean right in the morning and left in the afternoon. At night the moon casts them.
- Ice blue dot: The dot in Zone's icon changes color with each version.
- Clearer wording: Tighter text across setup, Home, Settings, Meetings and the keyboard.

## Speed and reliability
symbol: bolt

* Fast after a pause: The first dictation after your phone sat idle no longer waits.
- Quicker to open: The keyboard does less when it opens, so the first swipe lands sooner.
- Faster History: A long library opens faster and shows how little space it takes.
- Lighter on storage: While Zone keeps the mic ready, it writes to storage about 95% less.
- After a restart: Zone opens with your color, sounds and notes, not the defaults.
- CarPlay: Music stays on the car's speakers in stereo until you record.
- Storage on iOS 26: Removing the model frees all 1.2 GB. A nearly full iPhone can still load its model.
- After interruptions: The mic recovers after a call or meeting, and a double tap records once.
- Updated speech engine: Fixes for long dictations, model downloads and memory use in meetings.
