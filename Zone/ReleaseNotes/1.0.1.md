# 1.0.1
date: 2026-11-08
summary: Swipe typing, a space bar that moves the cursor, an emoji key, and meetings up to six hours long.

## New
symbol: sparkles

* Swipe typing (Beta): Glide across the letters and lift.
* Move the cursor with the space bar: Slide along the space bar to move the cursor right away, and drag faster to go further.
* Emoji key: An emoji key beside the space bar, with your recent ones first.
* Smarter autocorrect: Real words stay as typed, and missing apostrophes are added.
- Dictation follows Shift: When Shift is off, a dictation starts lowercase, even at the top of a field.
- @ handles (Experimental): Say "at sign" and a name to write a handle, or spell it letter by letter.
- Meetings up to six hours: Record for up to six hours.
- Light and Medium speech models: Choose Light or Medium for dictation.

## Fixes and polish
symbol: wrench.and.screwdriver

- New Mint and Rose accent colors.
- The keyboard's home button is the Z from the app icon. Hide it in Settings > Keyboard.
- Dictation writes Zayd, not Zaid, on every phone, while names like Zahid and Zaida stay as said. Names in My words also fix their possessive, like Zayd's.
- Meeting recording works in landscape.
- Turning dictation off cancels a start that is still in progress.
- Share on the Home card works again on iOS 26.
- The Discard key fits on smaller iPhones.
- The keyboard no longer learns from space bar slips.
- Fixed a freeze when automatic backup and history updates happened together.
- Fixed a crash after stopping an Action Button recording.
