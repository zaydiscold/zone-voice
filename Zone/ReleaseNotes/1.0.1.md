# 1.0.1
date: 2026-11-08
summary: Swipe typing, a space bar that moves the cursor, an emoji key, and meetings up to six hours long.

## Swipe typing
symbol: scribble.variable
tag: Beta
where: Settings > Keyboard > Swipe typing

Glide across the letters and lift. Zone uses the word before to pick the right one, and learns the words you keep. It is off until you turn it on.

## Move the cursor with the space bar
symbol: arrow.left.and.right

Slide along the space bar to move the cursor right away, and drag faster to go further. Emoji and accented letters stay whole.

## Emoji key
symbol: face.smiling
where: Settings > Keyboard > Emoji key

An emoji key beside the space bar, with your recent ones first. Hold an emoji to pick a skin tone, and Zone remembers it. It is off until you turn it on.

## Smarter autocorrect
symbol: textformat.abc
brief: Real words stay as typed, and missing apostrophes are added.

Real words stay as typed, missing apostrophes are added, and a missed space bar is split into two words. The keyboard remembers up to 5,000 of your words.

## Dictation follows Shift
symbol: shift
where: Settings > Keyboard > Follow Shift

When Shift is off, a dictation starts lowercase, even at the top of a field. Names and acronyms keep their capitals.

## @ handles
symbol: at
tag: Experimental
where: Settings > @ Handles

Say "at sign" and a name to write a handle, or spell it letter by letter. Zone keeps a list of your handles that you can edit.

## Meetings up to six hours
symbol: person.2.fill

Record for up to six hours. Zone saves the meeting at the limit or when storage runs low, and the Lock Screen says why. Pick the number of speakers when you transcribe again, and choose how the recorder looks.

## Light and Medium speech models
symbol: cpu
where: Settings > Speech model

Choose Light or Medium for dictation. Models show their real size, downloads can be cancelled, and removing a model keeps your notes, recordings and transcripts.

## Also in this version

- New Mint and Rose accent colors.
- The keyboard's home button is the Z from the app icon. Hide it in Settings > Keyboard.
- Dictation writes Zayd, not Zaid, on every phone, while names like Zahid and Zaida stay as said. Names in My words also fix their possessive, like Zayd's.
- Meeting recording works in landscape.
- Turning dictation off cancels a start that is still in progress.
- Share on the Home card works again on iOS 26.
- The Discard key fits on smaller iPhones.
- The keyboard no longer learns from space bar slips.
- Fixed a freeze when automatic backup and history updates happened together.
- Fixed a crash after stopping an Action Button recording.
