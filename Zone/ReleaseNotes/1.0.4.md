# 1.0.4
date: 2026-11-16
summary: Your words on your own computer, numbers written the way you type them, and a keyboard that knows today's words.

## Your words on your own computer
symbol: desktopcomputer
tag: Beta
where: Settings > Computers
brief: Send your dictations, notes and meetings to a computer you pair.

Send the text of your dictations, notes and meeting transcripts to a computer you choose. It is off until you pair one. Pair once by scanning a QR code from zone, a free, open source program for Mac, Windows and Linux. In Settings > Computers, Get Zone for your computer opens its download page (github.com/zaydiscold/zone-mcp) or shares it to the computer.

## Link: new items follow in seconds
symbol: arrow.triangle.2.circlepath
tag: Beta
brief: New items reach your computer in seconds, over any network.

After pairing, each new item reaches the computer a few seconds after you make it, over any network, not just the same Wi-Fi. Measured on an iPhone 15 Pro, a library of 1,764 items arrived in about a second.

## Encrypted, with no account and no server
symbol: lock.fill
brief: Text only, encrypted end to end. No account and no Zone server.

Only text is sent. Audio stays on the iPhone. The text is encrypted from the iPhone to the computer, with no account and no Zone server. A relay may pass the encrypted data along, and it cannot read it.

## Ask your AI agents what you said
symbol: text.magnifyingglass
brief: Search your words on the computer, or ask Claude Code, Codex or Cursor.

On the computer, zone gives you a searchable history page, a command line, and an MCP server for AI agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Cursor. Ask "what did I say on Wednesday between noon and five" and the answer comes from your own words.

## Flash: send with no network at all
symbol: qrcode
tag: Beta
where: Settings > Computers > No connection? Send by Flash
brief: No network? The computer's camera reads codes off your screen.

On a plane or a locked-down network, the phone plays a short piece of moving art with a window of color codes in it, and the computer's camera reads them. Each flash opens in one of three scenes: Dusk, Aurora or Ember. Black and white codes are there for older cameras. Flash has not been tried with a real camera yet, so expect rough edges.

## You decide what each computer gets
symbol: switch.2
where: Settings > Computers
brief: Pause a computer, or let it keep what you delete on the phone.

Send to this computer pauses and resumes one computer, and only what changed is sent when it is back on. Deleting something on the phone deletes the computer's copy at the next sync, unless you turn on Keep what I delete here for that computer. Privacy, History and the delete confirmations say which computers keep their copies.

## Send text to your computer
symbol: doc.on.clipboard
tag: Beta
where: Settings > Computers > Send text to your computer
brief: Paste a link or a code on the phone and it lands on your computer.

Paste or type a link, a code or anything you copied, and it is on your paired computer a moment later. The Send to Computer shortcut does the same from the Action Button or Back Tap, without opening Zone. If the computer cannot be reached, the text waits and goes with the next sync.

## Numbers the way you type them
symbol: number
brief: Larger numbers, times, amounts and percentages come out as digits.

Dictation writes larger numbers, years, times, amounts and percentages as digits: "forty five" is 45 and "two thousand" is 2,000. Small counts in a sentence stay words. Ordinals from 10th up are digits too, and Settings > Experimental > 1st, 2nd, 3rd as numbers does the same for the smaller ones.

## Spell it out loud
symbol: character.cursor.ibeam
brief: Spell a word letter by letter and Zone writes it as one word.

Spell a word letter by letter and Zone writes it as one word, like a name. Say "double" before a letter to repeat it. Abbreviations lose their periods: USA, not U.S.A.

## Check unsure words
symbol: text.badge.checkmark
tag: Beta
where: Settings > Experimental > Check unsure words
brief: Tap a word Zone may have misheard and pick the right one.

After a dictation, the keyboard shows the few words Zone may have misheard, beside Undo. Tap one to pick the right word, or keep it. It only flags a word when Zone is more likely wrong than right, so most dictations show none, and your choices teach it what to offer.

## California Mode
symbol: sun.max.fill
tag: Beta
where: Settings > Experimental > California Mode
brief: Takes the filler "like" out of your dictation. Off until you turn it on.

Takes the filler "like" out of your dictation and keeps the ones that mean something, such as "I like pizza" and "looks like rain". Light takes the clear ones, and Aggressive takes most of the rest. It is off until you turn it on, and History keeps the words as you said them.

## No more random capitals
symbol: textformat
brief: Everyday words on your contact cards stay lowercase.

Ordinary words that also appear on your contact cards stay lowercase, and real names keep their capital. Store and place names made of everyday words get their capitals once the whole name is typed.

## A keyboard that knows today's words
symbol: text.bubble.fill
brief: About 800 current words and names are no longer autocorrected away.

About 800 current words and names are no longer autocorrected away and can be swiped, and common texting shorthand is left alone. Your own words from My words complete as you type. A typo in one of them is corrected to it, with the capitals you saved.

## Swipe in search and address bars
symbol: magnifyingglass
brief: Swipe typing works in search bars and address bars.

Swipe typing now works in search bars and address bars, with a period key for web addresses. A word you pick from the swipe suggestions keeps its capitals the next time.

## A new Dynamic Island
symbol: capsule.fill
where: Settings > Dynamic Island
brief: A live waveform in your accent color, and four looks to choose from.

While Zone is ready the island is a small dot with a countdown ring; hold it and tap the power button to turn Zone off. While you dictate it shows a live waveform. Choose from four looks: Halo, Recorder, Caption and Ghost.

## History keeps everything until you delete it
symbol: clock.arrow.circlepath
where: Settings > Dictation history
brief: Dictations stay until you delete them, not just 30 days.

Dictations used to clear after 30 days. Now they stay until you delete them, and you can still pick 7 or 30 days. Notes are never affected.

## Also in this version

- New: tap Version in Settings to read the notes for every version. After an update, Zone shows what is new once.
- Settings is in a clearer order: Computers is under Setup, Your data holds history, backups and deletes, and Privacy is under About.
- Beside Start Flash, a warning for anyone whom flashing lights can affect: the codes change 20 times a second, in color and in black and white.
- Flash names each flash and tells the computer how many items it sends, so its page can check each one. Tap Done when the page says Done for that name.
- A smoother space bar cursor: slow slides no longer stall, up and down follow your finger line by line, and a diagonal moves both ways at once.
- Autocorrect reads the word before a typo, so "the fpod" becomes "the food", and fixes a missed space bar and a typo in the same word together.
- Smart punctuation, off by default in Settings > Keyboard: curly quotes, long dashes and the ellipsis as you type, as on Apple's keyboard.
- Select a misspelled word to see fixes for it, and Apple's spell checker fills an empty suggestion slot.
- The suggestion row swaps instantly as you type, without fading the style menu and mic.
- Caps lock dictation: tap Shift twice and the dictation is written in capitals.
- Say "quote" before and after words to put them in quotation marks.
- In casual styles, "smiley face" writes :) and "upside down smiley face" writes (:. Turn it off in Settings > Experimental > Faces.
- Formal and excited styles capitalize every sentence, and company names are spelled right in every style.
- Contacts no longer replace acronyms in dictation.
- No stray marker inside contractions and names.
- If the microphone stops delivering audio, Zone stops waiting after about 8 seconds and keeps what it heard.
- Keep microphone ready has 30 second and 1 minute choices.
- No freeze after copying text. Folder backups no longer pause the app, and write far less to storage.
- The keyboard keeps your accent color, layout and words the first time it opens after a restart.
- The keyboard uses far less memory over a long day of switching fields.
- Accent colors read better on light backgrounds.
- Home stats no longer run together on smaller iPhones.
- Labeling speakers in a meeting uses about half the memory.
