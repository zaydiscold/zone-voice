# 1.0.4
date: 2026-11-16
summary: Your words on your own computer, numbers written the way you type them, and a keyboard that knows today's words.

## New
symbol: sparkles

* Your words on your own computer (Beta): Send your dictations, notes and meetings to a computer you pair.
* Link: new items follow in seconds (Beta): New items reach your computer in seconds, over any network.
* Encrypted, with no account and no server: Text only, encrypted end to end. No account and no Zone server.
* Ask your AI agents what you said: Search your words on the computer, or ask Claude Code, Codex or Cursor.
- Flash: send with no network at all (Beta): No network? The computer's camera reads codes off your screen.
- You decide what each computer gets: Pause a computer, or let it keep what you delete on the phone.
- Send text to your computer (Beta): Paste a link or a code on the phone and it lands on your computer.
- Numbers the way you type them: Larger numbers, times, amounts and percentages come out as digits.
- Spell it out loud: Spell a word letter by letter and Zone writes it as one word.
- Check unsure words (Beta): Tap a word Zone may have misheard and pick the right one.
- California Mode (Beta): Takes the filler "like" out of your dictation. Off until you turn it on.
- No more random capitals: Everyday words on your contact cards stay lowercase.
- A keyboard that knows today's words: About 800 current words and names are no longer autocorrected away.
- Swipe in search and address bars: Swipe typing works in search bars and address bars.
- A new Dynamic Island: A live waveform in your accent color, and four looks to choose from.
- History keeps everything until you delete it: Dictations stay until you delete them, not just 30 days.

## Fixes and polish
symbol: wrench.and.screwdriver

- New: tap Version in Settings to read the notes for every version. After an update, Zone shows what is new once.
- Settings is in a clearer order: Computers is under Setup, Your data holds history, backups and deletes, and Privacy is under About.
- Beside Start Flash, a warning for anyone whom flashing lights can affect: the codes change 20 times a second, in color and in black and white.
- Flash names each flash and tells the computer how many items it sends, so its page can check each one. Tap Done when the page says Done for that name.
- A smoother space bar cursor: slow slides no longer stall, up and down follow your finger line by line, and a diagonal moves both ways at once.
- Autocorrect reads the word before a typo, so "the fpod" becomes "the food", and fixes a missed space bar and a typo in the same word together.
- Smart punctuation, off by default in Settings > Keyboard: curly quotes, long dashes and the ellipsis as you type, as on Apple's keyboard.
- Select a misspelled word to see fixes for it, and Apple's spell checker fills an empty suggestion slot.
- The suggestion row swaps instantly as you type, without fading the style menu and mic.
- Caps lock dictation: tap Shift twice and the dictation is written in capitals.
- Say "quote" before and after words to put them in quotation marks.
- In casual styles, "smiley face" writes :) and "upside down smiley face" writes (:. Turn it off in Settings > Dictation options.
- Formal and excited styles capitalize every sentence, and company names are spelled right in every style.
- Contacts no longer replace acronyms in dictation.
- No stray marker inside contractions and names.
- If the microphone stops delivering audio, Zone stops waiting after about 8 seconds and keeps what it heard.
- Keep microphone ready has 30 second and 1 minute choices.
- No freeze after copying text. Folder backups no longer pause the app, and write far less to storage.
- The keyboard keeps your accent color, layout and words the first time it opens after a restart.
- The keyboard uses far less memory over a long day of switching fields.
- Accent colors read better on light backgrounds.
- Home stats no longer run together on smaller iPhones.
- Labeling speakers in a meeting uses about half the memory.
