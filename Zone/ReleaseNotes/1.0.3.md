# 1.0.3
date: 2026-11-14
summary: A control that turns Zone on, voice notes that start reliably in the car, and a steadier Action Button.

## New
symbol: sparkles

* Toggle Zone: Add the Toggle Zone control to the Action Button, Control Center or the Lock Screen.
* Works in the car: Voice notes and dictation start reliably with CarPlay or car audio connected.
* A steadier Action Button: The Action Button starts a voice note on a locked iPhone, no passcode.
* Harder to lose a recording: The X on a recording asks before throwing it away, in the app and in the keyboard.
- Sentences that carry on: Finishing a sentence by voice no longer adds stray capitals or periods.

## Fixes and polish
symbol: wrench.and.screwdriver

- Opening Zone and starting a dictation no longer freeze the screen while the microphone gets ready.
- Fixed a rare crash when starting a dictation after hours of switching between AirPods, CarPlay and the phone's speaker.
- Words in My words no longer replace a common first name that sounds similar.
- Saying "at sign everyone" still writes @everyone without saving it to your handles.
- With the home button hidden, the keyboard's top-left key no longer runs into the rounded corner.
- Swipe typing, Paste and Suggestions apply the first time the keyboard opens after a relaunch.
- Labeling speakers for part of a meeting no longer listens past that part.
- Dictating a quote no longer types a stray marker where the quotation marks belong.
