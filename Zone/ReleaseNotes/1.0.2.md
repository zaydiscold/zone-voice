# 1.0.2
date: 2026-11-08
summary: A recording you can tuck away, a share card, and a cursor that moves between lines.

## New
symbol: sparkles

* Hide the listening screen: Hide the listening screen while you dictate and the recording keeps going.
* A share card: A card in Zone's own look, with a waveform drawn fresh each time you share.
* Move the cursor between lines: Slide up or down on the space bar to move between lines.
- Spoken corrections (Beta): Say "meet Tuesday, scratch that, Wednesday" and Zone types "meet Wednesday".

## Fixes and polish
symbol: wrench.and.screwdriver

- The keyboard's Zone button is the plain Z again, and looks right in light and dark mode.
- A repeated "I I think" comes out as "I think".
- Times read 4:30 instead of 4.30.
- Keep dictating while the keyboard is out of sight, for up to 15 minutes: Settings > Microphone and timing > Keyboard hidden.
- Settings are easier to scan.
