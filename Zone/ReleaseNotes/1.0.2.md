# 1.0.2
date: 2026-11-08
summary: Cleanup on your device, a recording you can tuck away, a share card, and a cursor that moves between lines.

## Clean up dictation on your device
symbol: sparkles
where: Home > Cleanup

Download Fluid Pico once, then review each change with the original kept for recovery. Turn on Automatically clean dictation there to clean up after every recording.

## Hide the listening screen
symbol: arrow.down.right.and.arrow.up.left

Hide the listening screen while you dictate and the recording keeps going. A strip at the top of every page shows the time, finishes with the checkmark, or opens the recording again.

## A share card
symbol: square.and.arrow.up

A card in Zone's own look, with a waveform drawn fresh each time you share. It shows your words per minute and the time you saved, and Settings has Share Zone with a friend.

## Move the cursor between lines
symbol: arrow.up.and.down
brief: Slide up or down on the space bar to move between lines.

Slide up or down on the space bar to move between lines, and the cursor keeps its column as you go. A fast drag no longer jumps across blank lines: it pauses at each line break with a tap you can feel.

## Spoken corrections
symbol: arrow.uturn.backward
tag: Beta
where: Settings > Experimental > Spoken corrections

Say "meet Tuesday, scratch that, Wednesday" and Zone types "meet Wednesday". It is off until you turn it on.

## Also in this version

- The keyboard's Zone button is the plain Z again, and looks right in light and dark mode.
- A repeated "I I think" comes out as "I think".
- Times read 4:30 instead of 4.30.
- Keep dictating while the keyboard is out of sight, for up to 15 minutes: Settings > Microphone and timing > Keyboard hidden.
- Settings are easier to scan.
