# 1.0.6
date: 2026-11-21
summary: Talk from the keyboard and stay in the app you’re in.

## Dictation
symbol: waveform

* Dictate without opening Zone: On iOS 26 or later, tap the keyboard mic. Your message stays on screen.
- Keyboard recognition: Choose Fluid Speech Mini or your own GPU (beta) in Settings > Zone keyboard.
- Standby: Turn on standby on Home for live words from Zone’s model. With standby off, the keyboard records.
- Restyle what you said: Tap Z and pick a text style. Tap Undo to get your words back.
- Hold space to talk (Beta): Let go to put the words at your cursor. A quick tap still types a space.
- Formal tidies up: Formal writes "going to", "want to" and "kind of" for "gonna", "wanna" and "kinda".
- Style preview: Settings > Text style shows one sentence written in every style.
- Tag a name: Turn on Say “at” to tag names in Settings. Say “at Kevin” to write @kevin.
- Speed up speech in Zone: Try it for speech and notes while Zone is on screen. Turn it on in Settings > Dictation options.
- Finish in Zone: If the keyboard saves a take it can’t transcribe, open Zone to finish it.
- Skip Apple’s speech model: In Settings > Zone keyboard, turn on Never use Apple’s speech model.
- Finish when quiet: In Microphone and timing, set a wait of 1 to 60 seconds that ignores the start sound.
- Changed draft: If you move to another field during dictation, tap Insert to put the words there.
- Recover keyboard audio: If a dictation is interrupted, open Zone to transcribe the saved recording.
- Numbers: In Settings > Numbers, choose digits or spell out numbers under ten.
- Matching numbers: Numbers in a list match, as in "8 or 9, maybe 7". "No one" stays in words.
- Spoken clock times: Say “meet at two fifteen” and each style writes 2:15. A number alone can stay a number.

## Keyboard
symbol: keyboard

- Swipe again: Delete a swiped word and try again to give a close match a turn.
- Swipe spacing: Messages keeps the space when you swipe a word and tap the next letter.
- Keyboard preview: See your keys in Settings > Personalize as you change their look.
- iPhone Duo: Open Duo flat in landscape. If Split is shown, tap it for two space bars. Tap Full to rejoin.
- Emoji predictions: Type or swipe pizza and tap 🍕 to swap the word. Press Space first to keep both.
* Emoji by name: Type :fi and tap 🔥. Or type :fire: for the same flame.
- One-handed keyboard: Move the keys toward your thumb on supported screens. Turn it on in Settings > Zone keyboard > One-handed.
- Space bar: Look for the faint Z in the corner. Slide across the key to move your cursor.
- When Zone doesn't answer: If Zone takes too long to open, the mic key comes back after ten seconds.
- Paste key stays visible: The Paste key keeps its color with an empty clipboard, in light and dark mode.
- Return to your app: If Zone is already recording your reply, tapping again won’t open it twice.
- Smoother swipes: The trail rounds tight turns and follows your fingertip.
- Ember accent: A warmer orange fills the app. The keyboard Z has a bright center and a burnt rim.
- Raised keys: The Z and style keys now sit up like the keys beside them.
- Texting shorthand: Short slang usually stays as typed. Zone can still fix a likely slip.
- Forget a typo: Swipe skips saved spellings that look like slips. Pick the suggested spelling to forget a slip.
- Undo after dictation: Tap Undo above the keys. If word choices cover it, close them first.
- Zone menu: Tap Z for Home, Notes, text styles and Settings. While recording, the keyboard key shows or hides the letters.

## Voice notes
symbol: note.text

- Action Button notes: Set the button to Voice note. Press it with Zone closed and speak when the mic starts.
- Set up the Action Button: Pictures show where to pick Voice note.
- Notes in a row: Read back-to-back notes on one page. Tap Copy all to copy the group.
- Time to think: Take a long pause between words. Silence alone won't end a voice note.
- Standby after a note: If standby was on, an Action Button note usually leaves it ready.
- Missed a note: Unlock your phone and check Home to see why the recording couldn’t start.
- Busy microphone: If iOS refuses the mic, Zone can try the Action Button recording again.
- Busy button: While you dictate, the Action Button and Voice note widget say Dictating instead of Off.
- Long notes: A note can keep recording past the island’s eight-hour limit.
- Pause on the card: Pause and resume a note from its recording card.
- Word count on Home: While the note card is open, Home counts the words you have said.
* Read a note: Tap a note on Home to read it. The keyboard waits until you tap Edit.
- Notes page: Your three newest notes sit on Home. Tap See all for the rest.
- If a note can't save: Check Home for any words or audio Zone could keep.
- Short notes: If no words come back, check Home for audio. A retry needs at least a quarter second.

## Meetings
symbol: person.2.fill

- Meeting waves: Lines cross as you talk. Try the looks in Meeting appearance.
- Familiar voices: Zone can suggest who spoke and check short turns again.
- Teach Zone your voice: Read and talk for about three minutes to help Zone recognize you in meetings.
* Merge two meetings: Zone matches voices it knows. If someone gets two labels, join them.
- Names survive a redo: If speaker labels fail, Zone keeps the names to match on your next try.

## Dynamic Island
symbol: capsule

- Your accent: The wave and meeting timer use your chosen accent.
- Standby island: Turn it off in Dynamic Island settings. Recordings still show a simple card with controls.
- Live times: See how long standby has been on. A countdown appears if you set an end time.
- Bring back Zone's island: During a meeting or standby, use the control to ask for the island again.
- If the standby island ends: Allow notices from Zone. Tap the notice to ask for the island again.
- Clear a recording card: Clearing the card stops and saves that take. Clearing a standby card can turn off the mic.
- Longer standby: On iOS 26, the island can stay for 16 hours after you last open Zone.

## Home and library
symbol: house

- Saved recordings: See how long each take lasted and how many times Zone tried it. Tap See all for the full list.
- Swipe across Home: Move one section at a time, or tap its key at the top.
- Step-by-step setup: Tap Set up one step at a time and follow the next prompt on Home.
* Clipboard history: Off until enabled. Turn it on in History to keep text and images you paste.
- Automatically save copies: Turn this on to save copies while Zone or its keyboard is visible.
- Private copies: To filter passwords, turn on “Skip things that look like passwords” in Clipboard history.
- Sent to a computer: Zone shows which computer got your copy. Delete everything can't recall a copy already sent.
- Keep your words: Back up My words, handles and learned spellings. Save or load a list in My words.
- Moving My words: On your next iPhone, restored spellings stay in the list of words the keyboard learned.
- Longest on the share card: Home dates your longest dictation, and the share card shows the record too.
- Version history: Settings > About > Version groups changes by topic, and What’s New shows the highlights.
- Shortcut help: Action Button and Shortcuts shows Zone's controls and where to find its shortcuts.
- Privacy: The Messages recent list and old recording logs now stay out of iPhone backups.
- History backups: History says which backups keep your dictations.
- Search: Notes and History can find similar words. Pick Everything to search meetings too.
- Keyboard setup on Home: Home shows the last setup the keyboard reported. It also counts words spoken.
- Home tabs: Swipe between Transcribe and Meeting. Zone frees space used by the old rewrite model.

## Look and feel
symbol: paintpalette

- Mic light: Your accent follows your voice on Home and in Listening. Pick Glow, Clear edge or Off in Personalize.

- Voice graphics: Scope is now Hex, and Lanes is Transcription Hero. Their saved selections carry over.
- Personalize in Settings: Pick the color scheme, accent and voice graphic, or open Dynamic Island and Meeting appearance.
- Two-tone accents: Each color has a light and dark tone. See both in Personalize and on the keyboard Z.
- Ember dot: The icon dot matches Ember’s Marigold tone on iPhone, Apple Watch and in Messages.

## Speed and reliability
symbol: bolt

- If standby stops: Allow notifications so Zone can tell you when standby stops early.
- Widgets during recording: Widgets refresh less often while the mic is recording.
