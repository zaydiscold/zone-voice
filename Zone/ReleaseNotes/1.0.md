# 1.0
date: 2026-11-05
summary: The first release. Dictation that runs on your iPhone, a keyboard with a microphone, voice notes and meetings.

## New
symbol: sparkles

* Dictation that stays on your iPhone: Your speech is turned into text on the phone itself, with no account and no server.
* A keyboard with a microphone: Add the Zone keyboard, tap its mic, talk, and tap again.
* Voice notes from the Action Button: Put Voice note on the Action Button or in Control Center.
* Meetings (Alpha): Record a long conversation and get a transcript with who spoke.
- Backup and export: Save your whole library to a file and bring it back later.
- Apple Watch and Messages: Write a note on your Watch and it lands in Zone on your iPhone.

## Fixes and polish
symbol: wrench.and.screwdriver

- Smaller recording and saving activities in the Dynamic Island.
- Settings are organized into sections.
- A Delete download button in the model library.
- Fixes for the Action Button and Control Center controls.
