# 1.0
date: 2026-11-05
summary: The first release. Dictation that runs on your iPhone, a keyboard with a microphone, voice notes and meetings.

## Dictation that stays on your iPhone
symbol: mic.fill

Your speech is turned into text on the phone itself, with no account and no server. Once the speech model is downloaded, it works in airplane mode.

## A keyboard with a microphone
symbol: keyboard
where: Settings > Keyboard

Add the Zone keyboard, tap its mic, talk, and tap again. The words go wherever your cursor is. It needs Allow Full Access so the keyboard and the app can pass text to each other.

## Voice notes from the Action Button
symbol: waveform
where: Settings > Action Button and Shortcuts

Put Voice note on the Action Button or in Control Center. Press to start, press again to stop, and the app you are in stays in front.

## Meetings
symbol: person.2.fill
tag: Alpha
brief: Record a long conversation and get a transcript with who spoke.

Record a long conversation and Zone writes the transcript after you stop, and can label who spoke. Play it back, edit the transcript and export it.

## Backup and export
symbol: externaldrive.fill
where: Settings > Backup and export

Save your whole library to a file and bring it back later. Automatic backups to a folder in Files or iCloud Drive are optional.

## Apple Watch and Messages
symbol: applewatch

Write a note on your Watch and it lands in Zone on your iPhone. In Messages, the Zone app puts a recent note or dictation into your draft.

## Also in this version

- Smaller recording and saving activities in the Dynamic Island.
- Settings are organized into sections.
- A Delete download button in the model library.
- Fixes for the Action Button and Control Center controls.
